🛒 Costumer Shopping Behavior Analysis

📌 Overview
This project analyzes costumer shopping behavior to identify purchasing trends, customer segments, loyalty patterns, and factors influencing purchase decisions.
The project uses Python, SQL, and Power BI to transform raw customer data into meaningful business insights and interactive visualizations.

 🎯 Business Problem
A leading retail company wants to better understand its customers' shopping behavior to improve:
- Sales performance
- Customer satisfaction
- Customer engagement
- Customer loyalty
- Marketing strategies
- Product strategies

The analysis focuses on factors such as demographics, product categories, discounts, reviews, seasons, payment preferences, and sales channels.

 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains consumer shopping and purchasing information that can be analyzed across different customer and transaction attributes.
Key areas analyzed include:
- Customer demographics
- Product categories
- Purchase behavior
- Discounts
- Reviews
- Seasons
- Payment methods
- Sales channels
- Customer loyalty

 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
 
- Python – Data cleaning, transformation & EDA
- Pandas – Data manipulation
- NumPy – Numerical operations
- Matplotlib & Seaborn – Data visualization
- SQL – Data analysis and business queries
- Power BI – Interactive dashboard and visualization
- Gamma – Presentation creation

 🔄 Project Workflow

 1. Data Loading
Loaded the raw consumer shopping dataset into Python for analysis.

 2. Data Cleaning
Performed data preparation and transformation, including handling data quality issues and preparing the dataset for analysis.

 3. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
Explored customer behavior and identified patterns across:

- Customer demographics
- Product categories
- Purchase trends
- Discounts
- Reviews
- Seasons
- Payment preferences

 4. SQL Analysis
Structured the data and used SQL queries to analyze:

- Customer segments
- Purchase behavior
- Customer loyalty
- Product trends
- Purchase drivers
- Business performance

5. Power BI Dashboard
Created an interactive Power BI dashboard to visualize important KPIs, trends, and customer behavior patterns.

 6. Report & Presentation
Created a project report and presentation summarizing the analysis, findings, and business recommendations.

 📈 Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive view of costumer shopping behavior.

It helps stakeholders explore:

- Customer trends
- Product category performance
- Purchase patterns
- Customer segmentation
- Discounts and purchase behavior
- Payment preferences
- Sales channel trends

💡 Key Results

The analysis helps identify patterns in costumer purchasing behavior and provides insights that can support:

- Better customer targeting
- Improved marketing strategies
- Customer retention initiatives
- Product strategy optimization
- Data-driven business decisions

> Detailed findings and recommendations are available in the project report and Power BI dashboard.
